Syllabus of Mains exam(Group B Non-Gazetted)

Paper Subject Questions Marks Duration Nature
Paper I Marathi (50 Qs / 100 M), English (30 Qs / 60 M), GK & IT (20 Qs / 40 M) 100 200 1 hours Merit (Common to all posts)
Paper II General Ability & Post-Specific Knowledge 100 200 1 hours Merit (Post-wise cut-off)
Total --- 200 400 2 hours Negative Marking: 1/4 (0.25)


Paper I- subjects covered

  • Marathi: Grammar (Sandhi, Samas, Prayog, Tenses), vocabulary, idioms, proverbs, and reading comprehension.
  • English: Grammar rules, error spotting, voice, vocabulary, idioms, and reading comprehension.
  • General Knowledge & IT: Right to Information Act (RTI 2005), Maharashtra Public Records Act, and fundamentals of Information & Communication Technology.

Paper II- subjects covered

  • Constitution of India & Governance: Fundamental rights, directive principles, judiciary, central & state executive relations.
  • Modern History & Social Reformers: 19th and 20th-century social reform movements in Maharashtra.
  • Geography & Environment: Environmental degradation, natural resource conservation, ecology, and climate variability.
  • Indian Economy & Public Finance: Planning, 1991 reforms, GST mechanism, foreign trade policy, and fiscal budgeting.
  • Post-Specific Laws & Systems:
    • PSI: Criminal laws (Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, BNSS, BSA).
    • STI: Maharashtra GST regulations, sales tax mechanisms, and commercial audit procedures.
    • ASO: Secretariat desk rules, office procedures, and administrative guidelines.
