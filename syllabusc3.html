Skill Test in Group C

Post Interview Requirement Skill/Typing test Final Selection Basis
Tax Assistant No Interview Yes (Marathi 30 WPM & English 40 WPM) Mains Marks + Qualifying Typing Test
Clerk-Typist No Interview Yes (Marathi 30 WPM or English 40 WPM) Mains Marks + Qualifying Typing Test
Industry Inspector / Technical Asst. No Interview Not Applicable Mains Marks Only (Out of 400)

This skill test is strictly qualifying in nature (marks are not added to your final Mains merit list, but passing it is mandatory to secure the job).



1.Exam Structure & Format

  • Test Duration: 10 Minutes per language test.
  • Passage Length:
    • Marathi: Approximately 300 words
    • English: Approximately 400 words
  • Keyboard Layout & Fonts:
    • English: Standard QWERTY layout.
    • Marathi: Remington Gail or Inscript layout using standard Unicode fonts (such as Mangal / Aparna).

2.Evaluation Rules & Error Tolerances

  • Permissible Error Limit: MPSC allows a maximum error tolerance of 7%. If your net errors exceed 7% of the total words, you are disqualified.
  • Editing Options: Backspace functionality is generally enabled, allowing corrections within the active line/word block during the test, though excessive corrections consume valuable time.
