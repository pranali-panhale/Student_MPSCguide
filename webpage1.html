Categories

The Maharashtra Public Services Commission (MPSC) classifies government positions into different groups based on responsibility, authority, and pay scale. Explore the categories below to understand the posts, their respective departments, and their core responsibilities.

Group A (Gazetted)- Senior Administrative & Police Posts

Post Department Role Overview
Deputy Collector (DC) Revenue Department / General Administration Assists the District Collector in revenue administration, law and order, and general governance.
Deputy Superintendent of Police (DySP) / ACP State Police (Home Department) Maintains law and order, investigates crime, and leads police units at the sub-divisional level.
Assistant Commissioner of State Taxes Finance Department (State Tax) Oversees state tax assessment, audits and enforcement at a senior level.
Tahsildar Revenue Department Handles revenue and administrative duties at the taluka level.
Deputy Director / Project Officer (Integrated Tribal Development Project) Tribal Development Department Manages tribal development projects and welfare schemes in tribal areas.
Deputy Registrar, Co-operative Societies Co-operation Department Supervises registration, audit and working of co-operative societies.
Deputy CEO / Block Development Officer (BDO) Rural Development (Zilla Parishad) Implements rural development schemes and assists in running the Zilla Parishad at the district or block level.
Assistant Director, Maharashtra Finance and Accounts Service Finance Department Manages financial administration, accounts and budgeting in government offices.
Chief Officer, Municipal Corporation / Council Urban Development Department Administers civic services and the day-to-day working of a municipal body.
Superintendent of State Excise State Excise Department Enforces excise laws and controls liquor licensing and revenue collection.
Education Officer, Maharashtra Education Service School Education Department Oversees schools, education programmes and administration at the district level.
Deputy Director of Industries (Technical) Industries Department Promotes industrial development and provides technical support to industries.
Child Development Project Officer Women and Child Development Department Runs child nutrition, health and early-education programmes (such as Anganwadi services) in a project area.
Assistant Director, Skill Development, Employment and Entrepreneurship Skill Development, Employment and Entrepreneurship Department Implements skill training, employment and entrepreneurship programmes.
Assistant Labour Commissioner Labour Department Enforces labour laws and settles industrial and workplace disputes.


Group B(Gazetted) - Mid level Administrative & Enforcement Posts

Post Department Role Overview
Naib Tahsildar Revenue Department Assists the Tahsildar in land revenue, records and taluka administration.
Deputy Superintendent, Land Records Revenue (Land Records) Manages land record administration at the district level.
Assistant Commissioner, State Excise State Excise Department Supervises excise enforcement and licensing in a region.
Deputy Superintendent, State Excise State Excise Department Enforces excise laws and controls illegal liquor in a district.
Assistant Regional Transport Officer (ARTO) Transport Department Handles vehicle registration, driving licences and Motor Vehicles Act enforcement.
Mantralaya Section Officer General Administration Department (Mantralaya) Processes files, drafts orders and assists senior officers in the state secretariat.
Assistant Block Development Officer Rural Development Department Assists the BDO in implementing rural schemes at the block level.
Chief Officer, Municipal Council Urban Development Department Administers the day-to-day working of a municipal council.
Accounting Officer, Maharashtra Finance and Accounts Service Finance Department Maintains accounts, audits and financial records of government offices.
Deputy Education Officer School Education Department Supervises schools and implements education programmes at the sub-district level.
Sub-Registrar, Co-operative Societies Co-operation Department Handles registration, inspection and supervision of co-operative societies.
Industry Officer (Technical) Industries Department Assists entrepreneurs with technical guidance and implements industrial schemes.
Government Labour Officer Labour Department Inspects workplaces and enforces labour welfare and wage laws.
Skill Development, Employment and Entrepreneurship Guidance Officer Skill Development, Employment and Entrepreneurship Department Offers career guidance and runs employment and skill programmes.
Superintendent, Inspector of Certified Schools and Institutions Women and Child Development Department Manages and inspects certified schools and institutions for children.


Group B (Non-Gazetted) - Police, Tax & Ministerial Posts

Post Department Role Overview
Police Sub-Inspector (PSI) State Police (Home Department) Investigates cases, maintains law and order, and runs a police station or beat.
State Tax Inspector (STI) Finance Department (State Tax) Carries out tax inspection, assessment, and enforcement and recovery work.
Assistant Section Officer (ASO) Mantralaya (General Administration) and MPSC Handles office files, noting and drafting in the state secretariat.
Sub-Registrar (Grade 1) / Stamp Inspector Revenue and Forest Department (Registration and Stamps) Registers property documents and checks stamp duty collection.


Group C (Non-Gazetted) - Clerical, Technical & Support Posts

Post Department Role Overview
Assistant Motor Vehicle Inspector (AMVI) Transport (Home Department) Inspects vehicles for roadworthiness and enforces motor vehicle rules.
Industry Inspector Industries Department Inspects and supports industrial units and implements industry schemes.
Excise Sub-Inspector State Excise Department Carries out field enforcement against illegal liquor and excise violations.
Technical Assistant Finance Department Provides technical and office support in departmental work.
Tax Assistant Finance Department Supports tax assessment, record keeping and tax office work.
Clerk-Typist Various government departments Does typing, filing, data entry and routine office work.
